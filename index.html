The setup

The rig. The bike. The trail.

Kulhavn Australia routes run on whichever rig actually suits the ground — never both at once. The 79 Series and Travelander go where range and camp matter most. The DR650 goes where a lighter, simpler bike gets further. The E-MTB handles the singletrack neither one can reach. Relive captures every ride.

01
79 Series + Travelander
LandCruiser 79 Series with Travelander camper. Carries camp, E-MTB, tools, camera gear, recovery kit and enough range to go places that don't have options.
02
Suzuki DR650
Light, simple, fixable anywhere and capable on dirt and bitumen equally. Runs on the routes where a smaller, nimbler bike makes more sense than the 79. Tracked on Relive.
03
E-MTB
For the trails nothing with an engine can reach. Carried on the Travelander when the 79 is on route. Every ride tracked on Relive and published.
The concept

Engineering-led. Field-tested. Zero filler.

Kulhavn is built on a simple idea: combine genuine engineering experience, infrastructure curiosity and remote field travel into content worth following — not manufactured adventure, not lifestyle filler.

The riding is real, so the content is real. Every route is tracked on Relive, documented as it happens, and posted without the sentimentality. Terrain does the talking.

Engineering credibility is the differentiator — always present, never shouted. Kulhavn sits beside the EES QLD legacy, not in front of it.

This is a long build. Audience and content come first. Structure follows once there's something worth structuring.

01
Field, not fashion
Motorbike touring, overland travel and E-MTB riding across terrain chosen for what it actually demands — not for how it photographs.
02
Content that compounds
Every route produces Reels, stats and route data. That content builds a specific audience over time — engineers, project professionals, technically-minded travellers.
03
Ancestry, in the background
Every country on the route has a genuine family connection behind it. It's part of why these routes exist — more of that story surfaces over time.
04
A community that grows
The people who follow Kulhavn are engineers, builders, travellers and operators interested in field work and properly built adventure — not passengers.
